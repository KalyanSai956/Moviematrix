Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Last updated: October 22, 2026

MovieMatrix is a daily Tollywood-themed crossword game. This policy explains how information may be collected when you visit the website, play a puzzle, or interact with its sharing features.

1. Information we collect

MovieMatrix does not require player accounts, registration, or login. We do not ask you to provide your name, phone number, or email address to play.

If you consent to analytics, our analytics provider may process information such as:

  • Page visits and interactions with game features.
  • Puzzle starts, submissions, and win or loss outcomes.
  • Approximate location inferred from network information, browser type, device category, referral source, and timestamps.
  • Anonymous browser identifiers used to estimate returning visitors.

The exact information collected depends on the analytics configuration enabled on the website. We aim to avoid sending puzzle answers, individual keystrokes, or directly identifying information to analytics.

2. Analytics and consent

MovieMatrix uses PostHog for website and product analytics, subject to your consent and the settings enabled on the site. Analytics helps us understand usage, improve the game, and measure engagement.

If you reject optional analytics, the site should not send optional analytics events. You can change your preference using the Privacy Settings control when available.

Essential functionality, such as displaying the puzzle, may operate independently of optional analytics.

3. Local storage

MovieMatrix may use browser storage to remember game progress, daily-play status, statistics, or your analytics preference. This information is stored in your browser and may be lost if you clear site data or use a different browser or device.

4. Third-party services

We use PostHog for analytics when enabled. PostHog may process information on its infrastructure according to its own privacy practices and applicable service terms.

When you click a WhatsApp sharing link, your browser may open WhatsApp or another application. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. MovieMatrix does not control their data practices.

Review the PostHog Privacy Policy for more information.

5. Sharing and social links

Sharing a score is optional. A share link or message may include puzzle-related results and the MovieMatrix website address. Review the message before sending it. Clicking an external social link may allow that service to collect information about your visit.

6. Data retention and security

Analytics information is retained according to the applicable provider settings and plan. Browser-stored information remains until it is cleared or overwritten. We take reasonable steps to limit collection and protect the services we operate, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

You can reject optional analytics, change your preference when the site's Privacy Settings control is available, or clear MovieMatrix site data in your browser settings.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other action concerning personal information. Requests can be sent using the contact method provided below.

8. Children's privacy

MovieMatrix is a general-audience puzzle game, not a service designed specifically to collect information from children. Please contact us if you believe information has been collected inappropriately.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game and its services evolve. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the MovieMatrix site operator through the official contact channel published on the website.

Before publication, replace the contact method above with a real, monitored email address or contact page. This policy must accurately reflect the analytics, consent, and storage behavior actually implemented on the live site.
